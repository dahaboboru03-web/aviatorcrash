📘 Game Rules

How to Play & Place Bets
  1. Each round has a short Betting phase, then the plane takes off and the multiplier grows.
  2. Place a bet of KSh 10.00 – KSh 10,000.00 per panel, with a maximum of one bet per panel per round. You can use two panels for two bets in the same round.
  3. Set an optional auto cash-out and the bet cashes out automatically at that multiplier.
  4. Cash out before the plane crashes. Your payout is stake × cash-out multiplier and is credited instantly.
  5. Once the round starts flying, a placed bet is locked and cannot be cancelled.
  6. During flight or after a crash you can queue a bet for the next round; the stake is taken when the queued bet activates, and if your balance is insufficient at that moment the queued bet is rejected.
  7. Manual bets can be cancelled during the betting phase and the full stake is returned (auto bets cannot be cancelled while auto is running).
Profile & VIP Status
  1. You can change your username and avatar anytime from your profile; they appear in chat, leaderboards and winner records.
  2. VIP status is earned by playing actively: place 5 or more bets within any 10 minutes.
  3. VIP players are highlighted with a gold ring and VIP badge in chat.
Account & Login
  1. Phone registration: Register with your phone number (07XX or 254XX format) and a password of at least 6 characters.
  2. One account per phone: A phone number can only be registered once. Duplicate registration is rejected.
  3. Login: Enter your phone and password to log in. Your balance, profile, and chat history are tied to your account.
  4. Forgot password: If you forget your password, tap "Forgot password?" on the login page, enter your registered phone, and wait for the admin to send you a verification code on WhatsApp.
  5. Reset code: Enter the 6-digit code and a new password on the reset screen. The code expires in 30 minutes and can only be used once.
  6. Logout: Tap your profile button and select Logout to sign out. You will need to log in again to play.
Deposits
  1. Deposit by M-Pesa to Paybill 542542, Account 03708400476150.
  2. After paying, paste the full M-Pesa confirmation message in the Deposit window and submit.
  3. Deposits stay pending until verified and approved by admin, then your balance is credited.
  4. Maximum deposit per request is KSh 150,000.00 and the minimum is KSh 10.00.
Withdrawals
  1. Minimum withdrawal is KSh 220.00 and maximum is KSh 70,000.00 per request.
  2. The requested amount is reserved from your balance immediately so it cannot be spent.
  3. A KSh 220.00 activation fee is paid to the same Paybill, then paste the M-Pesa confirmation message.
  4. Admin verifies the payment and approves the payout; the reserved amount is then released to your M-Pesa.
Freebets
  1. Freebets are awarded by admin and appear as a popup with the amount.
  2. Tap Claim to add the full amount to your balance.
  3. Each freebet has an expiry time (typically 5 minutes from award); unclaimed freebets expire and cannot be recovered.
  4. Closing the freebet popup forfeits the freebet — it is marked as lost, cannot be claimed, and a notice is sent to your inbox. Just refreshing the page never cancels a freebet.
  5. Admin also awards automatic freebets of KSh 50.00 to all players at the same time at random times (about once every 10 minutes), claimable for 60 seconds only.
Rain
  1. Rain is money shared in the chat by players or admin; the first players to claim get an equal share.
  2. Player rain is paid from the sender's balance; admin rain is separate and free to the admin.
  3. Per player KSh 10.00 – KSh 1,000.00, up to 10 players, total up to KSh 10,000.00.
  4. Rain lasts 60 seconds; claim it from the chat before it expires. Each player can claim a given rain only once.
  5. You cannot claim your own rain.
  6. Unclaimed player rain is forfeited to the system when it expires and is not returned to the sender.
  7. Admin also sends automatic rains at random times (typically one every 5 minutes: KSh 20.00 for 5 players).
Boosterpot
  1. Contribution: Every bet adds 1.01% of the stake to the Boosterpot.
  2. Target: Each event has a preset target. When the pot reaches it, the event is triggered.
  3. How to qualify: You must have a real bet (KSh 10.00 – KSh 10,000.00) in the round when the pot reaches its target. Players who stopped betting before that round are not eligible, even if they contributed earlier in the event.
  4. Cancelled bets: A cancelled bet is removed from the pot and you stop counting as an active eligible player, unless you still have another active or queued bet.
  5. Winner: One eligible player is picked at random. Each player gets one entry, regardless of how many bets they placed.
  6. Prize: The winner receives the full pot, credited to their balance, and a new event starts automatically.
  7. No eligible player: If nobody qualifies, a random system player wins and the pot resets.
Admin Account Adjustments
  1. Admin may add or deduct funds for account management: correcting errors, bonuses, promotions, refunds, penalties, or other verified adjustments.
  2. Every adjustment is recorded in your transaction history as Funds Added or Funds Deducted, with an admin reference.
  3. Your balance updates immediately when an adjustment is applied.
Chat Conduct
  1. Respect everyone: No insults, harassment, hate speech, sexual content, or threats in chat or direct messages.
  2. Bad language is blocked: Messages containing prohibited language are not sent.
  3. Warnings and mute: Each blocked message earns a warning. On the 3rd warning you are muted from public chat for 3 minutes.
  4. Repeated abuse: Admin may mute, restrict, or suspend accounts that keep breaking the rules.
  5. Message limits: Public chat messages are limited to 200 characters with a short cooldown between messages; direct messages are limited to 500 characters.
  6. No impersonation: Do not pretend to be admin or staff. Only messages marked as from Admin are official.
  7. Public visibility: Chat messages are visible to all players; never share passwords, PINs, or payment details in chat.
Chat Features & Message Tools
  1. Message menu: Press and hold a message (or tap the ⋮ button) to reply, react, forward, save, pin, report or delete.
  2. Reactions: Choose from 12 emojis. Each person can leave one reaction per message; tap the same emoji again to remove it.
  3. Reply: Quotes the message you are answering so replies stay in context.
  4. Forward: Shares a message again with a Forwarded label showing the original author. A picker lets you choose the destination: one or more players’ inboxes, the public chat, or both.
  5. Save: Bookmarks messages privately. Saved messages are visible only to you (up to 100) and can be cleared anytime from the chat toolbar.
  6. Pin: You can pin or unpin your own messages; admin can pin any message. Up to 3 pinned messages appear at the top of chat.
  7. Delete: You can delete your own messages and admin can delete any message. Deleted messages show Message deleted by … and cannot be restored.
  8. Report: Sends the message to admin for review with an optional reason. False or abusive reports may be restricted.
  9. Private reply: Opens a direct inbox conversation with that player instead of posting publicly.
  10. Typing indicator: While you type, other players see that you are typing in chat.
  11. Swipe shortcuts (inbox and public chat): Swipe a message right to reply, or left to open the forward picker.
Crash & Auto Cash-Out
  1. The round crashes at a random multiplier. A 1.00x crash is an instant crash: nobody can cash out and all bets placed in that round lose.
  2. Minimum auto cash-out is 1.10x. A value below 1.10x is corrected to 1.10x when you finish typing or place the bet.
  3. Auto cash-out cannot be set above 100.00x; the field is capped at 100.
  4. If you leave the auto cash-out field blank, no automatic cash-out is set and you may cash out manually at any moment you choose.
  5. Manual cash-out is allowed at any multiplier shown, including values below 1.10x.
  6. Maximum multiplier is 100.00x (the moon). If the plane reaches 100.00x, all active bets are automatically cashed out at 100.00x, whether or not an auto cash-out was set.
  7. Big cash-outs: Cash-outs at 80.00x or higher are announced in chat as Admin cash-out cards. The round's three highest cash-outs are kept — a bigger cash-out replaces the lowest card of that round.
  8. Automatic pins: The latest 2 cash-out cards are pinned at the top of chat automatically; when a new card arrives the oldest one is replaced. Admin can pin 1 extra message on top of these.
Fair Play & Security
  1. One account per person: Multiple accounts, bonus abuse, or collusion to influence outcomes may lead to suspension and forfeiture of winnings.
  2. No cheating: Do not exploit bugs, glitches, or automation tools. Report any bug you find instead of abusing it.
  3. Account safety: You are responsible for activity on your account; never share your session or device with others.
  4. Respectful identity: Usernames and avatars must be respectful; offensive names may be reset by admin.
  5. Fair rounds: Round crash points are generated randomly server-side and cannot be influenced by players.
Payment Verification
  1. Own number only: Use an M-Pesa number registered to you; payments from third parties may be rejected.
  2. One message, one use: An M-Pesa confirmation message can only be submitted once; duplicates are rejected.
  3. Accurate evidence: False, edited, or incomplete payment messages will be rejected and repeated abuse may restrict your payment options.
  4. Processing times: Deposits and withdrawals are processed after verification; incorrect details can delay approval.
  5. Reserved funds: A withdrawal amount stays reserved until it completes; if it fails, the reserved amount is returned to your balance.
Auto Bet
  1. Auto Bet places bets for you each round for a chosen number of rounds, optionally with automatic cash-out.
  2. You can stop Auto Bet at any time; it also stops automatically if your balance is insufficient.
  3. Auto bets cannot be manually cancelled while Auto Bet is running.
  4. Queued bets placed during a round are activated automatically when the next betting phase opens.
Freebet & Boosterpot Notes
  1. Freebets are personal, cannot be transferred, and can only be claimed once; expired or closed (lost) freebets cannot be restored.
  2. Boosterpot contributions are final per bet; cancelling a bet removes its contribution from the pot.
  3. A Boosterpot win is credited automatically; if you do not tap Collect, the win remains available for you in the popup.
General
  1. Admin decisions on disputes, adjustments and account restrictions are final.
  2. Rules may be updated at any time; continued play means you accept the current rules.
  3. Play responsibly — only bet what you can afford to lose.
